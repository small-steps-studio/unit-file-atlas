# Pro reports

* **Show Service Hardening**: A 15-point hardening checklist for services: non-root user, NoNewPrivileges, ProtectSystem, PrivateTmp and more.
* **Show Reliability Check**: Reliability mistakes: no restart policy, After= without Wants=, forking without PIDFile, no [Install], timers that skip runs.

The first Pro command starts a free 14-day trial on this computer. After the trial, Pro is a one-time purchase: USD 19 (Personal) or USD 39 per user (Business).
